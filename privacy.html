Privacy Policy for Text&JSON Compare

Last updated: January 19, 2026

1. Data Collection

Text&JSON Compare does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. All text and JSON data entered into the extension remains entirely on the user's local device.

The extension has no backend server, and no data is ever sent outside your machine.

2. Permissions Used

The extension requires minimal permissions to provide its core functionality:

  • No special permissions required – the extension operates entirely within its own popup window

Text&JSON Compare does not access your browsing history, tabs, or any website data. It simply provides a standalone tool for comparing text and JSON content.

3. Local-Only Operation

All comparison operations happen entirely within the browser. The text and JSON data you enter:

  • Is processed locally in your browser
  • Is never uploaded to any server
  • Is never stored persistently (cleared when you close the window)
  • Is never shared with any third party

4. Third-Party Services

This extension does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services. It does not make any network requests.
